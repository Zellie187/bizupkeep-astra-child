## 1.32.0
- **New "Customers" and "Invoices" tabs on My Bookkeeping**, backed by the `bizupkeep-bookkeeping` 1.7.0 plugin's new Invoicing module - the biggest feature gap versus Sage/QuickBooks/Xero closed: a client can now invoice their own customers (not BizUpKeep's clients - a separate, external party who is never a WP user) and download a customer's statement of account.
- Invoices are created as an editable Draft (never posted), then **Sent** - which posts `Debit Accounts Receivable / Credit revenue category [/ Credit VAT Output]` via `LedgerServiceInterface` directly (a different economic-event shape than Capture's simplified 2-line posting) and emails a real PDF invoice to the customer. Recording a **Payment** posts `Debit Bank/Cash / Credit AR` to clear the receivable. A Sent/Paid invoice is immutable like any other journal entry - only a Draft can be **Voided**.
- Invoice line prices are VAT-inclusive, split backward the same way Capture/Recurring already do - one consistent VAT mental model across every capture path in this plugin.
- **Real PDF documents** (not print-styled HTML) via a new `dompdf/dompdf` dependency - the ecosystem's first true PDF-generation library.
