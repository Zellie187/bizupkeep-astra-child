Our Commitment to Fairness

We understand that navigating business registrations can be complex. This policy is designed to be straightforward and fair. If you are ever unsure about your eligibility for a refund, please contact us before proceeding — we are happy to assist.

1 Overview

A2Z Business Administrators (Registration No. 2017/182869/07), trading as BizUpKeep, strives to deliver accurate, efficient, and professional services. We recognise, however, that circumstances may arise where a client needs to cancel a service or is dissatisfied with an outcome. This Refund Policy sets out the terms under which refunds may be granted.

This policy must be read together with our Terms and Conditions and Privacy Policy, all of which form part of our service agreement with you.

Because we provide professional administrative services that involve time, expertise, and in many cases the payment of non-recoverable government fees to the CIPC on your behalf, refunds are evaluated on a case-by-case basis according to the stage of processing and the circumstances of the request.

3 Nature of Our Services

It is important to understand the nature of the services we provide before placing an order:

  • Our services are professional administrative services, not the sale of a physical product. Once work has commenced, time, expertise, and resources have been expended.
  • Many of our services require the upfront payment of non-refundable government fees to the CIPC, which we pay on your behalf. These fees cannot be recovered once submitted.
  • The outcome of applications submitted to the CIPC is at the discretion of the CIPC. We cannot guarantee approval of any registration, name reservation, or filing.
  • Delays and rejections caused by CIPC system issues, government processing backlogs, or inaccurate information provided by the Client are outside our control.
By placing an order and making payment, you acknowledge and accept the above constraints and the refund terms that follow.

5 Cancellation Before Processing

If you cancel your order before A2Z Business Administrators has commenced any work on your application and before any government fees have been paid on your behalf, you are entitled to a refund of your service fee, less a non-refundable administrative handling fee.

Administrative Handling Fee

A handling fee of R150.00 (incl. VAT) applies to all cancellations to cover payment processing, administration, and bank charges incurred. This fee is non-negotiable and non-refundable.

To qualify for a pre-processing refund, you must notify us in writing by email to billing@bizupkeep.co.za as soon as possible after placing your order. We will confirm whether processing has commenced within 1 (one) Business Day of receiving your cancellation request.

Act quickly — once your application enters our processing queue, we may have already begun preparing documents or submitting information to the CIPC.

6 Cancellation After Processing Has Commenced

Once A2Z Business Administrators has commenced work on your application, refunds are assessed based on the stage reached:

Stage 1 — Documents Being Prepared

Work has begun but no government fees have been submitted. A partial refund of the service fee may be granted, less a proportional charge for work completed and the R150 handling fee.

Stage 2 — Application Submitted to CIPC

Government fees have been paid and your application has been submitted. No refund is available for government fees. Our service fee is non-refundable at this stage, except where an error on our part is identified.

Stage 3 — CIPC Processing Underway

Your application is actively being processed by the CIPC. No refunds are available at this stage. Any concerns should be raised so we can assist in following up with the CIPC on your behalf.

Stage 4 — Service Successfully Completed

Your registration or filing has been confirmed and documents delivered. The service has been fully rendered and no refunds are available after successful completion.

7 Government and Third-Party Fees

Certain services require the payment of fees to the CIPC or other regulatory bodies. These fees are paid directly on your behalf as part of the service and are strictly non-refundable once submitted, regardless of the reason for cancellation.

Government fees are set by and payable to the CIPC. A2Z Business Administrators has no control over these fees, cannot recover them once paid, and accepts no liability for any increases in government fees after a quotation has been issued to you.

⚠ Non-Refundable Government Fees:

Once government fees have been submitted to the CIPC on your behalf, these amounts are permanently non-refundable under any circumstances, including CIPC rejection, client-initiated cancellation, or change of mind.

Where government fees are included in a bundled service price, the non-refundable government fee component will be clearly communicated to you prior to payment. On request, we will provide an itemised breakdown of our service fee versus the government fee portion.

8 CIPC Rejections and Application Failures

We cannot guarantee that every application submitted to the CIPC will be approved. Rejections may occur for various reasons, and our refund position differs depending on the cause:

8.1 Rejection Due to Client-Provided Information

If your application is rejected because of inaccurate, false, incomplete, or non-compliant information provided by you — including an already-registered company name, duplicate director ID numbers, or non-compliant documentation — no refund will be issued for government fees or our service fee. We will advise you on the steps required to resubmit, which may involve a new order and additional fees.

8.2 Rejection Due to CIPC System Errors or Processing Issues

If your application is rejected solely due to a CIPC system error, technical fault, or internal CIPC processing issue unrelated to any error on our part or yours, we will resubmit your application at no additional charge. If resubmission is not possible or you choose not to proceed, our service fee will be refunded. Government fees already paid remain non-refundable.

8.3 Rejection Due to Our Error

If your application is rejected due to a verified error made by A2Z Business Administrators — such as incorrect data entry or submission of wrong documentation by us — we will correct and resubmit at no cost to you. If the error cannot be corrected, a full refund of all fees (including government fees where recoverable) will be issued. Please refer to Section 9 for further detail.

9 Service Errors by A2Z Business Administrators

We hold ourselves to a high standard of accuracy and professionalism. If we make an error that directly causes your application to fail or that results in demonstrable harm, we will take full responsibility. Where a service failure is directly attributable to us, your options are:

  • Re-performance: We will redo or correct the service at no additional cost to you — this is our preferred first remedy.
  • Full refund: If re-performance is not possible or you do not wish to proceed, we will issue a full refund of all amounts paid to us. Government fees will be refunded to the extent they are recoverable from the CIPC.

To report a service error, please contact us in writing at info@bizupkeep.co.za with your order reference and a description of the issue. We will investigate and respond within 3 (three) Business Days.

10 Non-Refundable Situations

No refund will be issued in any of the following circumstances:

  • The service has been successfully completed and documents have been delivered to you
  • Cancellation is requested after government fees have been submitted to the CIPC on your behalf
  • The application was rejected due to inaccurate, incomplete, or fraudulent information provided by you
  • The rejection or delay was caused by a change in CIPC requirements, government policy, or legislation after your order was placed
  • You changed your mind about the entity type, company name, or business structure after processing commenced
  • Delays caused by your failure to provide required documents or information timeously
  • Dissatisfaction with CIPC processing times, government turnaround times, or outcomes outside our control
  • Services delivered correctly that did not achieve the outcome you expected due to third-party decisions
Please note: A change of mind does not constitute grounds for a refund once processing has commenced. We strongly encourage you to confirm all requirements before placing an order.

11 How to Request a Refund

All refund requests must be submitted in writing. We do not accept verbal refund requests. Please follow these steps:

Step 1 — Submit Your Request in Writing

Email your refund request to billing@bizupkeep.co.za with the subject line: "Refund Request — [Your Order Reference Number]".

Step 2 — Include the Required Information

Your email must include your full name, order reference number, the service requested, date of payment, reason for the refund request, and any supporting documentation.

Step 3 — Await Acknowledgement

We will acknowledge receipt of your request within 1 (one) Business Day and confirm the current processing stage of your application.

Step 4 — Review and Decision

We will assess your request against this policy and communicate our decision — including the amount (if any) to be refunded and the reason — within 5 (five) Business Days.

Step 5 — Refund Payment

Approved refunds will be processed within 7 (seven) Business Days of approval, returned to the original payment method or via EFT to your nominated bank account.

12 Refund Processing and Timeline

Once a refund has been approved, the following timelines apply:

  • Card payments (Stripe): Refunds are returned to the original card and typically appear within 5 – 10 Business Days, depending on your bank.
  • EFT / Bank Transfer payments: Refunds are processed via EFT to your nominated bank account within 7 (seven) Business Days of approval. You will be asked to provide your banking details.
Please Note

A2Z Business Administrators is not responsible for delays caused by your bank or payment provider in processing refunds. If you have not received your refund within the stated period, please check with your bank before contacting us.

All refunds are issued in South African Rand (ZAR). Currency fluctuation is not grounds for an additional refund.

13 Your Consumer Rights (CPA)

Nothing in this Refund Policy is intended to limit, exclude, or override any rights you have as a consumer under the Consumer Protection Act 68 of 2008 (CPA) or any other applicable South African legislation. Under the CPA, you have the right to:

  • Receive services performed in a manner and quality that persons are generally entitled to expect
  • Receive a remedy (repair, replacement, or refund) where a service fails to meet the required standard
  • Cancel a fixed-term agreement with reasonable notice, subject to a reasonable cancellation penalty
  • Fair, honest, and transparent dealing in all transactions

If you believe your consumer rights have not been upheld, you may contact the following bodies:

Consumer Dispute Resolution Options

🏛 National Consumer Commission (NCC): www.thencc.gov.za | 0860 266 786

⚖ Consumer Goods & Services Ombud (CGSO): www.cgso.org.za | 0860 000 272

🏚 National Consumer Tribunal (NCT): www.thenct.org.za

We encourage you to contact us first. We are committed to resolving all complaints fairly and promptly.
