2 Who We Are

A2Z Business Administrators is an online business services company specialising in company registrations and related compliance services in South Africa. We act as the Responsible Party as defined under POPIA, meaning we determine the purpose of and means for processing your personal information.

Our services include but are not limited to:

  • Non-profit company and close corporation conversions

4 Lawful Basis for Processing

We process your personal information on one or more of the following lawful grounds as provided under POPIA:

  • Consent: You have given us specific, informed, and voluntary consent to process your personal information for one or more specific purposes.
  • Contract: Processing is necessary for the performance of a contract to which you are a party, or to take steps at your request prior to entering into a contract.
  • Legal Obligation: Processing is necessary for compliance with a legal obligation to which we are subject (e.g. CIPC regulations, FICA, tax legislation).
  • Legitimate Interest: Processing is necessary for pursuing our legitimate interests or those of a third party, provided these are not overridden by your rights and interests.
  • Vital Interests: Processing is necessary to protect your vital interests or those of another person.

9 Security of Personal Information

We take the security of your personal information seriously and implement appropriate technical and organisational measures to protect it against unauthorised access, loss, destruction, or disclosure. These measures include:

  • Secure Socket Layer (SSL) / TLS encryption on all data transmissions
  • Secure, access-controlled cloud storage environments
  • Role-based access controls limiting staff access to personal information on a need-to-know basis

  • Strong password and multi-factor authentication policies
  • Staff training on data protection obligations
Notwithstanding the above, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. In the event of a data breach that is likely to harm your rights and interests, we will notify you and the Information Regulator as required under section 22 of POPIA, within the prescribed timeframes.

10 Your Rights as a Data Subject

As a data subject under POPIA, you have the following rights in respect of your personal information held by us:

🔍 Right of Access

Request confirmation of whether we hold personal information about you and obtain a copy, free of charge, once per year.

✏️ Right to Correction

Request that we correct, update, or complete any personal information that is inaccurate, irrelevant, outdated, incomplete, or misleading.

🗑️ Right to Deletion

Request deletion or destruction of your personal information where we no longer have a lawful basis to retain it, subject to legal obligations.

🚫 Right to Object

Object to the processing of your personal information on grounds relating to your particular situation, including objecting to direct marketing.

↩️ Right to Withdraw Consent

Where processing is based on consent, you may withdraw it at any time. Withdrawal does not affect prior lawful processing.

⚖️ Right to Complain

Lodge a complaint with the Information Regulator of South Africa if you believe we have processed your information in violation of POPIA.

Information Regulator (South Africa)

🌐 Website: www.inforegulator.org.za

📧 General: inforeg@justice.gov.za

📧 Complaints: POPIAComplaints@inforegulator.org.za

To exercise any of your rights, please contact us using the details in Section 12. We will respond within 30 (thirty) days of receipt of your request.

11 Cookies and Website Tracking

Our website makes use of cookies and similar technologies to enhance your browsing experience, analyse website traffic, and improve our services. Cookies are small text files stored on your device when you visit our website.

We use the following types of cookies:

  • Essential Cookies: Required for the basic functioning of our website and cannot be disabled.
  • Analytical / Performance Cookies: Help us understand how visitors interact with our website (e.g. Google Analytics).
  • Functional Cookies: Remember your preferences and settings to enhance your experience.

You may disable cookies through your browser settings; however, doing so may affect the functionality of certain areas of our website.
